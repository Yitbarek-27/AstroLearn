🌌 Galaxies

Explore the enormous systems of stars, gas, dust, and dark matter that fill the universe. Discover the different types of galaxies, learn about our Milky Way, and see how galaxies form, evolve, interact, and organize themselves across the cosmic web.

📈 Intermediate 📚 8 Lessons 🏆 Certificate

📖 About This Course

Galaxies are among the largest structures in the universe. They contain billions or even trillions of stars together with gas, dust, dark matter, and other cosmic objects. In this course, you will explore how galaxies are classified, how their structures differ, and how astronomers study them across enormous distances. You will also discover our home galaxy, the Milky Way, the role of supermassive black holes, and the processes that shape galaxies throughout cosmic history.

🎯 What You Will Learn

  • What galaxies are and what they contain
  • How astronomers classify different types of galaxies
  • The structure and properties of spiral galaxies
  • How elliptical and irregular galaxies differ
  • The structure and location of our Milky Way
  • How supermassive black holes are connected to galaxies
  • How galaxies form and evolve over cosmic time
  • How galaxies are organized into groups, clusters, and the cosmic web
