🌌 About AstroLearn

Your Journey Through Space Begins Here.

AstroLearn is an astronomy education platform created to make astronomy easier, more engaging, and accessible to students who want to explore the universe.

🚀 Explore Courses 🧠 Take the Quiz

🌠 Our Mission

AstroLearn aims to help students discover astronomy through clear lessons, interactive learning, quizzes, and carefully organized courses.

Whether you are just beginning to learn about the night sky or already interested in astrophysics and space science, AstroLearn provides a place to continue your journey.

🔭 What You Can Learn

AstroLearn currently offers courses covering major areas of astronomy and space science.

  • 🌌 Introduction to Astronomy
  • ☀️ The Solar System
  • ⭐ The Life Cycle of Stars
  • 🌠 Galaxies
  • 🕳️ Black Holes
  • 🌌 Cosmology

📚 How AstroLearn Works

Each course is divided into eight lessons. You can study the lessons at your own pace and mark each lesson as completed.

After completing all eight lessons, the final examination becomes available. Students who achieve at least 70% on the exam can unlock their course certificate.

🏆 Certificates

AstroLearn certificates are awarded after completing an entire course and successfully passing its final examination.

Each certificate includes the student's name, course name, completion date, and a unique certificate ID.
