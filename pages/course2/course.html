📖 About This Course

The Solar System is our home in space. In this course, students will travel through the planets, explore the Sun, understand planetary systems, and discover the smaller objects that shape our cosmic environment. This course introduces astronomy concepts through scientific explanations, discoveries, and real space missions.

🎯 What You Will Learn

  • How the Solar System formed 4.6 billion years ago
  • The structure and activity of the Sun
  • The characteristics of all eight planets
  • The importance of moons, asteroids, and comets
  • The mysteries of the outer Solar System
  • How scientists explore planets using spacecraft
