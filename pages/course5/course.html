🕳️ Black Holes

Explore one of the most mysterious and extreme phenomena in the universe. Discover how black holes form, how they affect space and time, how astronomers detect objects that cannot be seen directly, and what happens near their powerful gravitational boundaries.

📈 Intermediate 📚 8 Lessons 🏆 Certificate

📖 About This Course

Black holes are among the most fascinating objects in modern astronomy. Their gravity can become so intense that, beyond a certain boundary, not even light can escape.

But black holes are not simply cosmic vacuum cleaners or mysterious holes floating through space. They are real astronomical objects whose existence can be studied through their effects on nearby stars, gas, radiation, and even the structure of space and time.

In this course, you will explore how black holes form, what the event horizon really means, how matter behaves around them, and how astronomers observe objects that cannot be seen directly.

🎯 What You Will Learn

  • What a black hole actually is and why its gravity is so extreme
  • How some black holes form from massive stars
  • What the event horizon represents
  • How matter behaves around black holes
  • What accretion disks are and why they can become extremely hot
  • How black holes affect nearby stars and galaxies
  • What supermassive black holes are
  • How astronomers detect and study invisible black holes
  • How black holes help us understand gravity and the universe
