🚀 Lesson 1: What is Astronomy?

Astronomy is the scientific study of objects and events beyond Earth. It helps us understand planets, stars, galaxies, and the universe itself.

🌌 What is Astronomy?

Astronomy is the scientific study of everything beyond Earth's atmosphere. It explores planets, moons, stars, galaxies, nebulae, black holes, and the universe itself. Unlike astrology, astronomy is a science based on observation, mathematics, and physics.

Astronomers use powerful telescopes, satellites, and space probes to understand how celestial objects form, move, interact, and evolve over billions of years.

🔭 What Do Astronomers Study?

Astronomers investigate a wide range of objects including planets, stars, comets, asteroids, galaxies, black holes, and even invisible matter such as dark matter and dark energy.

Their discoveries help us understand Earth's place in the universe and answer questions about the origin and future of everything that exists.

💡 Did You Know?

The light from the Sun takes about 8 minutes and 20 seconds to reach Earth, even though it travels at nearly 300,000 kilometers every second.

🌍 Why Astronomy Matters

Astronomy has contributed to many technologies we use today, including GPS satellites, digital cameras, wireless communication, and advanced computer systems. It also inspires curiosity and encourages scientific thinking.

📝 Key Takeaways

  • Astronomy is the scientific study of the universe.
  • It investigates stars, planets, galaxies and other celestial objects.
  • Astronomers rely on telescopes and scientific evidence.
  • Astronomy helps answer some of humanity's biggest questions.
