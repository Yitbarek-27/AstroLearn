🌌 1. Understanding Astronomy

Astronomy is the scientific study of objects and events beyond Earth. It uses mathematics, physics, and technology to understand planets, stars, galaxies, and the universe.

📜 2. History of Astronomy

Ancient civilizations observed the sky to create calendars and understand seasons. Later scientists such as Copernicus, Galileo, Kepler, and Newton changed humanity's understanding of the universe.

🌠 3. Branches of Astronomy

Astronomy includes many fields such as planetary science, stellar astronomy, cosmology, astrophysics, and observational astronomy.

💡 4. Light and Space

Light carries information from distant objects. By studying different wavelengths of electromagnetic radiation, astronomers can discover the properties of stars, planets, and galaxies.

📏 5. Measuring the Universe

Astronomers use special distance units because space is extremely large. The astronomical unit measures distances inside our Solar System, while the light-year measures distances between stars and galaxies.

🔭 6. Telescopes and Observation

Telescopes allow scientists to collect information from distant objects. Ground-based and space telescopes have helped humanity discover planets, galaxies, and the history of the universe.

⭐ 7. Astronomers and Discoveries

The work of scientists throughout history has built modern astronomy. Their discoveries helped explain planetary motion, gravity, galaxies, and the evolution of the universe.

💡 Final Reflection

Astronomy is not only about studying space. It is about curiosity, exploration, and asking questions about our place in the universe.

📝 Course Summary

  • Astronomy explains the universe through science.
  • Stars, planets, galaxies, and black holes reveal the history of space.
  • Technology allows humans to explore beyond Earth.
  • Scientific discoveries continue to expand our understanding.
