🌌 Course Overview

Cosmology is the scientific study of the universe as a whole. It asks some of the biggest questions in science: How did the universe begin? How has it changed over time? What is the universe made of? How large is it? And what might happen to it in the distant future?

Throughout this course, you will learn how observations of galaxies, stars, cosmic background radiation, and large-scale structures allow astronomers and physicists to reconstruct the history of the universe.

🚀 What You Will Learn

🌠 How the universe began and developed.

🔭 How astronomers observe the expanding universe.

🌌 What the Big Bang theory actually means.

📡 How cosmic microwave background radiation provides evidence about the early universe.

🌑 What dark matter is and why scientists study it.

✨ What dark energy may have to do with cosmic expansion.

🕸️ How galaxies form the large-scale cosmic web.

⏳ How the universe may evolve in the distant future.
