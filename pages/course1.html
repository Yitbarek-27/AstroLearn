Begin your journey into astronomy and discover how humans study the universe. Explore stars, planets, galaxies, light, telescopes, space measurement, and the discoveries that changed our understanding of the cosmos.

📖 About This Course

Astronomy is the scientific study of everything beyond Earth. From nearby planets and stars to distant galaxies and the structure of the universe, astronomy helps us understand our place in the cosmos.

In this introductory course, you will build a foundation in astronomy by learning how the field developed, how astronomers classify different branches of science, how light carries information across space, and how scientists observe objects that are billions of light-years away.

You will also discover the history of important astronomical discoveries and learn how modern astronomers use telescopes and other instruments to investigate the universe.

🎯 What You Will Learn

  • What astronomy is and how it differs from other sciences
  • How ancient civilizations studied the sky
  • The major branches of modern astronomy
  • How light and the electromagnetic spectrum help astronomers study space
  • How astronomers measure enormous cosmic distances
  • How telescopes collect and analyze light
  • The discoveries of famous astronomers throughout history
  • How the major ideas of astronomy connect together
